Monument: Adalaj Stepwell (Adalaj ni Vav), Adalaj village, Gandhinagar district, Gujarat
Recorded by: Team KathaGraph, note A (summary of popular and tourism accounts)

Popular accounts say the stepwell was built in 1498 by Queen Rudabai, wife of Rana Veer Singh of the Vaghela dynasty.
It is five storeys deep and built of sandstone.
The carving mixes Indo-Islamic architecture with Hindu and Jain motifs.
