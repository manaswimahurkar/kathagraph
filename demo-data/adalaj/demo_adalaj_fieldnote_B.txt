DEMO FIELD NOTE (written by the KathaGraph team for the hackathon demo; based on public sources, not an original site visit)

Monument: Adalaj Stepwell, Adalaj, Gandhinagar, Gujarat
Recorded by: Team KathaGraph, note B (summary of published references to the site's inscription)

Published references to the Sanskrit inscription at the stepwell date it to Vikram Samvat 1555, which corresponds to 1499 CE,
and name Rudabai as the queen who had it built.
We have not seen or photographed the inscription ourselves.
