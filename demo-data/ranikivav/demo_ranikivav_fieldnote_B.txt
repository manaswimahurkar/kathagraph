DEMO FIELD NOTE (written by the KathaGraph team for the hackathon demo; based on public sources, not an original site visit)

Monument: Rani ki Vav, Patan, Gujarat
Recorded by: Team KathaGraph, note B (notes from a local guide's account)

The guide told us the stepwell was started around 1050 CE, during the reign of King Bhima I himself,
and finished later by his queen. He said it was commissioned by the king, not built only as a memorial after his death.

Seven storeys of steps lead down to the water. The carved panels show Vishnu's ten avatars, apsaras and nagakanyas.
Built of sandstone. Patan was the capital of the Solanki dynasty.

The guide also mentioned a small gate at the lowest level said to lead to a tunnel towards Sidhpur, now blocked.
We could not confirm this from any document.
